Corgi Runner

Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026.

Corgi Runner has no advertising, no tracking and no account of its own. The game does not send data to the developer.

Data on your Apple TV

The game keeps the ten best runs, the names that you type after a run, and your best score in its settings on your Apple TV. When you remove the game, the Apple TV removes them.

iCloud

The game keeps your best score in your iCloud, so that your Apple TVs have the same one. The names and the list of runs do not go to iCloud.

Game Center

When you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends the score of each run to the world leaderboard. Apple shows it with your Game Center name. Apple keeps this data under its own privacy policy.

Children

The game does not ask for personal data. A name after a run is optional and can be any word.

This site

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and has no trackers.
